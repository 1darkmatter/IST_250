About

Creative Technology and Cinematic Systems

I am David Magnabosco, a Creative Technologist, designer, and systems builder working across AI video, visual design, web development, and creative automation.

My process follows a simple rule: do not prompt an aesthetic, prompt a production. Instead of relying on vague visual language, I define how a scene is constructed, photographed, lit, performed, and edited.

I use node-based workflows, constraint-driven prompting, image generation, video generation, and frame chaining to create cinematic sequences with stronger continuity and more deliberate visual direction.

Accessibility is part of the structure. As a Deaf creator, I approach storytelling, communication, and interaction with a strong awareness of visual clarity, readable information, and experiences that do not depend entirely on sound.
